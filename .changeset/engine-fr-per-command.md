French prompts are worded per command where French AutoCAD words them so: new prompt ids (`alignedSecondExtensionOrigin`, `polygonFirstEndpointOfEdge`, `polygonSecondEndpointOfEdge`, `enterThirdCvPointOr`, `peditMultipleOption`), the labels French lists otherwise at one prompt (SPLINE's `Tangence fin`, EDITSPLINE's `Par Défaut`, PEDIT Multiple's `Typeligne gén`), and the answers French writes against the colon (the arrays' counts, spacings, type, method and alignment, COTANG's first prompt, SPLINE's third CV point). LIST words a fitted polyline's POLYLINE and VERTEX lines with keys apart from an LWPOLYLINE's (`listPolylineStartingWidth`, `listPolylineEndingWidth`, `listVertexEndingWidth`, `listVertexBulge`, `listVertexStartAngle`, `listVertexEndAngle`, `listLwRadius`), which French writes a column apart and in words of their own; the other languages read as before.
