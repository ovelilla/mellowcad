`@mellowcad/engine/locales/de`, German's catalog, is an entry point: its interface and the command language of German AutoCAD 2027 (`LINIE`, `STUTZEN`, `[Schließen/Zurück]`). A language may write a prompt's verb after its keyword list (`commands.promptTails`, German `Höhe oder [2Punkt] angeben <10.0000>:`), with the default before it where its AutoCAD does (`promptForms`, `beforeTail`); new prompt ids for the solids' prompts asked with no default (`boxHeightNoDefaultOr` and three more) and PYRAMID's inscribed radius (`pyramidInscribedRadiusOr`), and `promptKeywords` slots German words per prompt, filled in every language with the words it already used.
