The application menu takes AutoCAD's two columns: New, Open, Save, Save As and Drawing Utilities on the left, each with its 32 px glyph, and on the right the page of the entry under the pointer — its items with AutoCAD's descriptions in each language — or Open Documents, with the page's language at its foot. Drawing Units is now Drawing Utilities ▸ Units. The catalog's `TitleBar` has new keys for the pages (`newHint` … `openDocuments`), and `units` is now AutoCAD's `Units`. The application button no longer has a tooltip.
