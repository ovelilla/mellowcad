3DMOVE (`3M`), as AutoCAD 2027's: MOVE's prompts, and off the plan the 3D Move gizmo at the set's centre, an arrow or a square taken constraining the move to that axis or plane at `Specify move point or [Base point/Copy/Undo/eXit]:`. `THEME` gains the gizmos' colours (`gizmoX`, `gizmoY`, `gizmoZ`, `gizmoHot`, `gizmoHotFill`, `gizmoBase`, `gizmoBaseEdge`).
