The ViewCube rests as AutoCAD's: the cube, its ring and the UCS menu alone, translucent. With the pointer on it, the focus in it or one of its menus open it turns opaque and shows Home at its top left, the roll arrows at its top right, a face view's triangles and, at its bottom right, its menu's arrow, which opens the menu a right-click opens. Home no longer stands above the cube, so the cube and the navigation bar sit higher; the cube's menu hands the keys back to the drawing when it closes.
