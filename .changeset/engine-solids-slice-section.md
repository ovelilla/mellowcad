SLICE (SL) and SECTION (SEC), PRESSPULL on a solid's faces and a solid's faces, edges and vertices selected with Ctrl+click, as AutoCAD 2027 does them — in English, Spanish (CORTE, SECCION) and Brazilian Portuguese (FATIAR, CORTE). SLICE cuts the solids of a set by a plane given by two points, three, a point and its normal, the view, the UCS's planes or a planar object, keeping a side or both, the halves keeping the solid's properties; SECTION adds a region of each solid where the plane cuts it, on the current layer. PRESSPULL extrudes a planar face clicked, or offsets one Ctrl+clicked, its neighbours carried on to meet it. Ctrl+click at `Command:` selects a face, an edge or a vertex, Ctrl+Space walks to the one behind, and the selection holds them apart from whole objects (`Selection.subobjects()`), with AutoCAD's highlight and red grips. The engine's kernel fixes a boolean of faces that coincide with their shared edges run opposite ways, such as two boxes stacked. The editor's rail gains Slice beside the booleans. New catalog keys: `interface.tool.slice`, `interface.tool.section`, `commands.names.SLICE`, `commands.names.SECTION`, their keywords and `keywordLabels.SLICE`, the plane's prompts (`selectObjectsToSlice`, `sliceStartOr`, `sectionFirstPointOr`, `planeSecondPoint`, `sliceObject`, `sectionObject`, `sliceSurface`, `planeSectionPoint`, `planeZAxisPoint`, `planeViewPoint`, `planeXYPoint`, `planeYZPoint`, `planeZXPoint`, `planeFirstPoint`, `planeThirdPoint`, `sliceSideOr`, `sliceBoth`, `presspullOffsetOr`), their messages, `promptForms.sliceStartOr`, `answerForms.presspullOffsetOr` and `format.promptWithDefaultOpen`.
