Polygon meshes (`PolygonMeshEntity`, among `Entity`'s types): 3DMESH, in every language; PEDIT's mesh prompts — Edit vertex with Next, Previous, Left, Right, Up, Down and Move, Smooth surface by SURFTYPE, SURFU and SURFV (new drawing settings `surfaceType`, `surfaceU`, `surfaceV`), Desmooth, Mclose and Nclose, Undo; the mesh drawn, picked and snapped by its edges, a smoothed one's fitted mesh; its vertex grips, the Properties palette's Geometry, Mesh and Fit/Smooth rows; MOVE, COPY, ROTATE, SCALE, MIRROR (its rows reversed, as AutoCAD's), STRETCH, LIST and EXPLODE into 3D faces; OFFSET, BREAK, FILLET, CHAMFER, TRIM, EXTEND, DIVIDE and UCS OBject refuse it as AutoCAD does. A DXF opens and saves its polygon meshes, smoothed or not. Drawing file version 65.
