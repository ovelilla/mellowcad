The ribbon's Home tab replaces the tool palette: Draw, Modify, Annotation, Layers, Block, Properties, Utilities and Clipboard in AutoCAD 2027's rows and sizes, large buttons with 32 px icons and small ones, split buttons that keep AutoCAD's behaviour and the member picked last (the engine's, for the session), each panel's slide-out with its push pin, and every word AutoCAD's own in each language. The slot `ToolRail` is gone and `Ribbon` takes the ribbon's band; the catalog's part `ToolRail` is gone, `Ribbon` is new, and `TitleBar`'s `layer`, `color`, `lineweight` and `linetype` moved to `Ribbon`, with the Layers and Properties controls that left the title bar.
