3D faces (`Face3dEntity`, among `Entity`'s types): 3DFACE with its Invisible option, in every language; the face drawn, picked and snapped by its visible edges, continuous whatever its linetype; its corner grips, the Properties palette's Current Vertex, Vertex X, Y and Z and Edge rows; MOVE, COPY, ROTATE, SCALE, MIRROR (its corners reversed, as AutoCAD's), STRETCH and LIST; EXPLODE, OFFSET, BREAK, FILLET and CHAMFER refuse it as AutoCAD does. A DXF opens and saves its 3DFACE objects. Drawing file version 57.
