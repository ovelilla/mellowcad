`@mellowcad/engine/locales/ru`, Russian's catalog, is an entry point: its interface and the command language of Russian AutoCAD 2027 (`ОТРЕЗОК`, `ОБРЕЗАТЬ`, `[Замкнуть/Отменить]`). Text is measured in Cyrillic with Arial's own widths (`textFontMetrics.ts` reads U+0400–04FF), where a Cyrillic letter was an average glyph.
