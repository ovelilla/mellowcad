Wipeouts (`WipeoutEntity`, among `Entity`'s types) and WIPEOUTFRAME (`DocumentSettings.wipeoutFrame`): WIPEOUT with its Frames and Polyline options, in every language; the wipeout masks what is drawn before it with the background, its frame shown, hidden or shown and not plotted as WIPEOUTFRAME says; picked anywhere it masks, snapped on its frame; its vertex grips; MOVE, COPY, ROTATE, SCALE, MIRROR, STRETCH, EXPLODE and LIST; OFFSET, AREA, BREAK and DIVIDE refuse it as AutoCAD does. A DXF opens and saves its WIPEOUT objects and WIPEOUTFRAME. Drawing file version 58.
