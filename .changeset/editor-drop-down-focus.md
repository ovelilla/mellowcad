A pick in a drop-down of the ribbon, the Properties palette or the Layer Properties Manager gives the drawing the keys once its list has closed: the next key types a command, where it used to move the drop-down's value on or open its list again. The ribbon's piece is unmounted while RIBBONCLOSE or Clean Screen has closed it, so a slide-out pinned open no longer stays over the title bar, and the wheel over a list the ribbon opened no longer scrolls the panels under it.
