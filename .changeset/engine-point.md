Point objects (`PointEntity`, among `Entity`'s types), drawn as the drawing's PDMODE and PDSIZE say (`DocumentSettings.pointMode`, `pointSize`), with POINT, DIVIDE, MEASURE, PTYPE (its Point Style dialog: `state.pointStyleDialog`, `setPointStyleDialog`, `applyPointStyle`, `state.pointMode`, `state.pointSize`), the PDMODE and PDSIZE variables and the Node object snap (`SnapType.Node`); a DXF opens and saves its points, `$PDMODE` and `$PDSIZE`.
