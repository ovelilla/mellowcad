How the history writes an answer after a prompt is the language's: the new `commands.answerForms` says, by prompt id, whether it goes past the colon's space (`[Multiple]: M`) or against the colon where the language's prompt ends without one, with `format.answeredTight`, and `EngineText.answerForm` reads it. DIMSPACE's prompts and -DIMSTYLE's redefine now write `Select base dimension:50,-40` in every language, as their consoles do; Brazilian Portuguese writes PEDIT's, MREDO's, ARRAYPATH's main prompt and ARRAYRECT's rows at Spacing the same way, and Spanish the unit cell. ARRAYPATH's main prompt (`selectGripToEditPathArrayOr`) and ARRAYRECT's rows at Spacing (`specifyDistanceBetweenRowsOfCell`) have prompt ids of their own; Brazilian Portuguese words Rows' `Especificar a distância entre linhas`, and its Ortho and Grid toggles `<Ortogonal ativado>` and `<Grade ativado>`, as its desktop does.
