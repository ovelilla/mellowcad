The ViewCube takes AutoCAD's automatic size and place: its centre 80 px from the drawing's edge and 110 from its top, whole on a drawing some 708 px high or more, and smaller, place and all, on a shorter one (the box is `--cube-size`, which a host rendering the cube elsewhere may set; 9rem by default). The navigation bar sits against the drawing's edge under it. The command line's F2 history opens some 300 px high, and it and the temporary prompt lines start at Recent Commands, as AutoCAD's do.
