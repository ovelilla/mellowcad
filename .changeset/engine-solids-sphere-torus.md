SPHERE and TORUS, as AutoCAD 2027 asks them: the centre by a point, 3P, 2P or Ttr, the radius or Diameter, TORUS's tube radius, 2Point or Diameter, measured from the ring's point last given, a negative ring making an apple or a lemon, with their refusals and shared defaults. The solid kernel's `BrepSurface` may be a `sphere` or a `torus`, a face with no edge or with an apple's or a lemon's poles; area and volume are exact for them, `closedSurfaceReach` bounds them and `brepAnchor` places a solid with no vertex. `SpherePrimitive` and `TorusPrimitive` join `SolidPrimitive`. Their isolines are ISOLINES' meridians and parallels, and Center finds their centre from any of them. A failed Nearest override now says `No found for specified point.`, as AutoCAD does. Drawing file version 46. The editor's Modeling drop-down gains Sphere and Torus.
