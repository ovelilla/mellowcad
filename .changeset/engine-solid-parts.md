A solid's faces, edges and vertices are a set MOVE, COPY, ROTATE, SCALE and ERASE take, as AutoCAD 2027's: picked in SUbobject or with Ctrl at `Select objects:`, or selected before the command. MOVE, ROTATE and SCALE reshape the solid with the faces round them carried on, COPY copies the whole solid, ERASE leaves its geometry and drops what made it.
