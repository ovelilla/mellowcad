Grips take AutoCAD's colours and sizes: a square grip is 11 px across in GRIPCOLOR 150's blue (`#007fff`) with a grey edge, the one under the pointer GRIPHOVER 11's pale red and the one taken GRIPHOT 12's red, both 13 across. `THEME.grip` and `THEME.gripHot` change value, and `THEME` gains `gripHover`, `gripEdge` and `gripHotEdge`; the crosshair's pick box is drawn 7 px across (PICKBOX 3). The editor's accent, which is the grips' blue, follows.
