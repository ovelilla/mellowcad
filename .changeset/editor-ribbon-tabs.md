The ribbon gains the Insert, Annotate, 3D Tools and View tabs, in AutoCAD 2027's order and rows: every tool the tool palette offered has its button again. View holds the palette toggles the title bar had (its Styles toggle is gone: View's Palettes has none), the UCS Icon and View Cube toggles, the Visual Styles gallery, the UCS buttons and Navigate. The Attribute Display drop-down (Home and Insert) sets ATTMODE, and the colour lists end in a row that opens the browser's colour picker (`More Colors...` on the ribbon, `Select Color...` in the Properties palette), where a swatch did. The catalog's `TitleBar.commandLine` and `Properties.customColor` are gone; `Ribbon.moreColors`, `Properties.selectColor` and the new tabs' words are new.
