MASSPROP, as AutoCAD 2027 reports it: a set's solids and regions, each summed as one body, in the current UCS — mass, volume, bounding box, centroid, moments and products of inertia, radii of gyration and the principal moments with their directions; a region off the XY plane, its area, perimeter, box and centroid —, word for word in English, Spanish (PROPFIS) and Brazilian Portuguese (PROPMASS), then `Write analysis to a file?`, whose Yes hands an `.mpr` file to the `FileTransport` (`save` receives it as it receives a drawing; the browser's names its download's type by the extension). The kernel integrates every face exactly. The editor's rail leaves MASSPROP out, as AutoCAD's ribbon does: it is typed. New catalog keys: `interface.tool.massprop`, `commands.names.MASSPROP`, `commands.keywords.MASSPROP`, `commands.prompts.massPropWriteFile`, the `massProp*` messages.
