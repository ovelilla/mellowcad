One array selected at `Command:` shows AutoCAD's Array contextual tab, its kind's: a grid's Columns, Rows and Levels with Between and Total, a ring's Items, Between, Fill, Rotate Items and Direction, a path's Items, Between, Method and Align Items, each edit applied to the array as its Properties row would, with nothing written and a step U names nothing. ARRAYPATH's Array Creation tab gains the count's toggle before Items, Fill entire path: lit at first, it greys Items and keeps all that fit as Between changes, as AutoCAD's does under Measure. `ContextualTabId` gains `arraySelectionRectangular`, `arraySelectionPolar` and `arraySelectionPath`, `ContextualFieldId` gains `columnTotal` and `rowTotal`, and `ContextualToggleId` gains `fillPath`. The editor's catalogs gain the tab's name and the two Totals' words.
