The ribbon's tooltips are AutoCAD's: after a second at rest the button's name, its description, the command and `Press F1 for more help`, and two seconds later the extended tooltip with its paragraph, every word AutoCAD's own in each language (Arabic ours); a split button's arm and a button that only lists have theirs, and none opens over an open list. The alias leaves the tooltip. While a tooltip shows, F1 asks Help for its command: the engine gains `setTooltipHelp`. The editor's catalog gains `Ribbon.tips`, `tipTitles`, `tipCommands`, `extended` and `pressF1`.
