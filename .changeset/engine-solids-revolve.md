REVOLVE, as AutoCAD 2027 asks it: the set of closed profiles and MOde as EXTRUDE's, the axis by two points, a line or a polyline's straight segment (Object), or the UCS's X, Y or Z, then the angle (a whole turn for 0 or a turn or more), STart angle, Reverse and EXpression; each profile becomes a solid whose faces are the ones ACIS makes (cylinders, cones, planes, spheres, tori, revolutions of ellipses and splines), exact in volume and MASSPROP, with AutoCAD's refusals for a profile across the axis, an axis square to it or of no length. LIST shows `Solid type = Revolve` with its start angle, angle and direction vector; `SolidSweep` gains its `revolve` kind.
