OFFSET draws a copy of an object parallel to it, at a distance you type or through a point you pick: a line gives a parallel line, a circle or an arc a concentric one, a polyline an outline inside or outside it, an ellipse or a spline a spline that follows it. As in AutoCAD, it takes one object at a time: you give the distance once, then pick an object and the side to put its copy on, as many times as you like. Enter ends it.

- **Command:** `OFFSET`
- **Aliases:** `O`
- **Panel:** [Modify](/docs/commands#modify)
- **Try it:** [Open the editor](/draw), type `O` and press Enter.

## Options [#options]

### `Through` (`T`) [#through]

Instead of a distance, each copy passes through a point you pick or type. It is the first prompt's default, so Enter there chooses it until a distance has been given; from then on Enter repeats the last distance, in that drawing.

### `Erase` (`E`) [#erase]

Asks whether to erase each source object once it is offset, `Yes` or `No` (the default). With `Yes`, the copy replaces the object. The choice is kept for the rest of the session, and the next OFFSET says it in its first line.

### `Layer` (`L`) [#layer]

Asks where the copies go: on the `Current` layer, or on the `Source` object's (the default). Kept for the session too.

### `Exit` (`E`) [#exit]

Ends the command, as Enter does at the object prompt.

### `Multiple` (`M`) [#multiple]

At the side prompt: keep offsetting the same object without picking it again, each copy one distance further out from the last, until Enter or `Exit`.

### `Undo` (`U`) [#undo]

Takes back the last copy, whichever object it came from, and asks for an object again. With no copy left to take back, it says so.

## What it does not do yet [#missing]

OFFSETGAPTYPE 1 and 2: the corners of an offset polyline are always closed as with 0, the segments extended to meet. A spline's or an ellipse's offset is within AutoCAD's tolerance, but its knots fall where MellowCAD puts them, not where AutoCAD does. Through on a spline shows X and Y in its tooltip, not the distance.

## Questions [#questions]

### What is the shortcut for OFFSET in AutoCAD?

`O`, then Enter or Space. The full name works too, and `_OFFSET` in any language of AutoCAD.

### Why can't I offset an object?

OFFSET offsets lines, arcs, circles, 2D polylines, ellipses and splines. It refuses text, multiline text, block references, dimensions and hatches with `Cannot offset that object.`, and so it does when the distance would shrink a circle, an arc or a polyline to nothing. An object on a locked layer cannot be picked, and a 3D polyline is passed over.

### How do I offset the same line several times?

Pick the line, then type the letter of `Multiple` at the side prompt: each pick on that side makes one more copy, each a distance beyond the last. Or keep the default and pick the newest copy each time.

### How do I put the offset copies on the current layer?

Choose `Layer` at the first prompt, then `Current`. From then on every copy goes on the layer that is current, until you choose `Source` again.
