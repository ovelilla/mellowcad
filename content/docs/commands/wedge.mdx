WEDGE draws a 3D solid wedge: a box whose top slopes from one end down to the other. It asks what [`BOX`](/docs/commands/box) asks: the first corner, the other corner, then the height. The tall end is at the first corner, and the slope falls along the UCS's X to the other corner's side; a corner at a smaller X turns it round. The height rises along the UCS's Z, a negative one goes down. The length, width and height are [`BOX`](/docs/commands/box)'s defaults, which Enter takes. The wedge lies on the current layer, and Undo takes it back in one step.

[`LIST`](/docs/commands/list) shows its position, length, width, height and rotation, and the Properties palette lets you type them. Its grips are [`BOX`](/docs/commands/box)'s. It is a 3D solid for [`UNION`](/docs/commands/union), [`SUBTRACT`](/docs/commands/subtract) and the rest of MellowCAD's 3D, which is solids only and still partial; [`EXPLODE`](/docs/commands/explode) turns it into a region per face.

- **Command:** `WEDGE`
- **Aliases:** `WE`
- **Panel:** [Draw](/docs/commands#draw)
- **Try it:** [Open the editor](/draw), type `WE` and press Enter.

## Options [#options]

### `Center` (`C`) [#center]

At the first prompt: asks for the centre, then a corner, `Cube` or `Length`. The wedge is centred on the point, in height too, its tall end opposite the corner given.

### `Cube` (`C`) [#cube]

At the other corner: asks for a length and makes a wedge with every side that long. Unlike [`BOX`](/docs/commands/box)'s, it runs along the UCS's X, backwards when negative.

### `Length` (`L`) [#length]

At the other corner: asks for the length, along the UCS's X, then the width, along its Y; a negative one runs backwards. A point gives only its distance, never its heading.

### `2Point` (`2P`) [#2point]

At the height: asks for two points, and the height is the distance between them.

## What it does not do yet [#missing]

[`BOX`](/docs/commands/box)'s: Move, Rotate, Scale and Mirror from a hot grip, the 3D commands of its own, the dotted dimensions while you give the corner, and the visual styles [`VSCURRENT`](/docs/commands/vscurrent) lacks. In plan, the Endpoint snap under the tall end finds the top's corner, where AutoCAD finds the base's.

## Questions [#questions]

### What is the shortcut for WEDGE in AutoCAD?

`WE`. Type it and press Enter or Space, then give the first corner, the other corner and the height: `0,0`, `60,40` and `25` draw the session's wedge.

### Which way does the wedge slope?

Down from the first corner's end, along the UCS's X. To turn it, give the other corner at a smaller X, which turns it 180°, or rotate it afterwards with [`ROTATE`](/docs/commands/rotate).

### How do I draw a triangular prism?

A wedge is one: a right triangle at each end, swept along the width. For any other triangle, draw it closed with [`PLINE`](/docs/commands/pline) and [`EXTRUDE`](/docs/commands/extrude) it.

### How do I draw a wedge from its centre?

Choose `Center` and give the centre point, then a corner, `Cube` or `Length`: the wedge is centred on the point, in height too.
