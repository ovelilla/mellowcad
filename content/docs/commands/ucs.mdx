UCS sets the user coordinate system: an origin and X and Y axes anywhere in space, so its XY plane can tilt. The coordinates you type are read in it, and what you draw lies in its XY plane: with the origin moved to a box's top, a circle centred at `30,20` lies on the top. It first says the current UCS's name — `*WORLD*`, `*NO NAME*` or the name it was saved under — then asks for the new origin or an option; Enter there goes back to World. A point is the origin, with its Z, and a point without a Z lies at the current elevation. Then a point on the positive X axis and a point on the XY plane, read in the current UCS: Enter at either keeps the axes as they are. One point moves the UCS; three turn it any way.

The commands then work in the UCS's XY plane: [`LINE`](/docs/commands/line) and [`CIRCLE`](/docs/commands/circle) draw on it, and [`BOX`](/docs/commands/box) and [`EXTRUDE`](/docs/commands/extrude) rise from it along its Z. Every object keeps its own plane when the UCS changes, and [`PLAN`](/docs/commands/plan) looks straight down on the current UCS. The UCS is saved in the drawing, and each run is one undo step. MellowCAD's 3D is solids only, and still partial.

- **Command:** `UCS`
- **Panel:** [Inquiry & view](/docs/commands#inquiry)
- **Try it:** [Open the editor](/draw), type `UCS` and press Enter.

## Options [#options]

### `Face` (`F`) [#face]

Listed as AutoCAD 2027 lists it, to align the UCS with a face of a solid. MellowCAD asks for the face, but does not align to one yet: every pick finds none.

### `NAmed` (`NA`) [#named]

Saved UCSs, by name. `Save` stores the current one, asking `Yes` or `No` before it replaces a name already saved; `Restore` makes a saved one current, its name typed in any case; `Delete` takes names, with commas and wildcards; `?` lists them, each with its origin and axes. A name holds spaces, but not `<>/\":;|,=*?` or a backquote: `Invalid UCS name.`.

### `OBject` (`OB`) [#object]

Aligns the UCS with an object you pick: a line, circle, arc, ellipse, polyline, spline, text, multiline text, attribute definition or block reference. The origin and the X axis go where AutoCAD puts them for each kind, and the Z is the object's own normal, so the UCS lies in the object's plane. An object on a locked layer can be picked. A hatch says `This object does not define a coordinate system`, and UCS ends with the UCS as it was.

### `Previous` (`P`) [#previous]

Goes back to the UCS before, up to ten back in a session, named ones and orthographic ones among them. With none left: `No previous coordinate system saved.`.

### `View` (`V`) [#view]

Turns the XY plane square to your line of sight, X along the screen's horizontal, keeping the origin: text drawn after it reads flat on the screen of a 3D view.

### `World` (`W`) [#world]

Back to the world coordinate system, which never moves. It is the first prompt's default, so Enter there chooses it, and it sets the elevation back to 0 too.

### `X` [#x]

Turns the UCS about its X axis: 90 degrees by default, an angle typed, or a point that gives it. A positive angle turns it by the right-hand rule, so 90 stands the XY plane up on the old X axis.

### `Y` [#y]

Turns the UCS about its Y axis, as `X` does about X.

### `Z` [#z]

Turns the UCS about its Z axis, as `X` does about X: 30 turns X and Y 30 degrees counter-clockwise, seen from above.

### `ZAxis` (`ZA`) [#zaxis]

A new origin, Enter keeping the current one, then a point on the positive Z axis: the XY plane stands square to it, its X kept level with the world's XY plane. Two points that are one say `Points are coincident`. Its `Object` takes a line, an arc, an open polyline or a spline: the origin at the end nearest the pick, Z along it pointing away.

## Questions [#questions]

### How do I reset the UCS to World in AutoCAD?

Run UCS and press Enter: `World` is the default. To look straight down on the drawing again, run [`PLAN`](/docs/commands/plan) and choose `World`.

### How do I rotate the UCS?

Run UCS, choose `Z` and type the angle: `30` turns X and Y 30 degrees counter-clockwise. `X` and `Y` tilt the XY plane up, 90 degrees by default.

### What is the difference between the UCS and the WCS?

The world coordinate system (WCS) never moves; the user coordinate system (UCS) is the one you move and turn. At the start they are the same, and UCS says the current one is `*WORLD*`. Coordinates you type are read in the UCS, and what you draw lies in its XY plane.

### How do I draw on a face of a 3D solid?

Move the UCS onto it. On a box's top, its origin alone is enough: UCS, then a corner of the top, then Enter, as the session does. For a side, give three points: the origin at a corner, a point along an edge for X, and one more on the face for Y. Then draw: what you draw lies on the face.
