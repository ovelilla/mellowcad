ZOOM makes the view larger or smaller; nothing in the drawing moves or changes size. Its first prompt takes a corner, which starts a window, a scale factor, or an option. Typed with an apostrophe, `'ZOOM` or `'Z`, it works inside another command, which goes on after it. The mouse wheel zooms in and out about the cursor, and a double-click of the wheel button is ZOOM Extents. Each ZOOM is an undo step, consecutive ones a single step, so `U` takes the view back too.

- **Command:** `ZOOM`
- **Aliases:** `Z`
- **Panel:** [Inquiry & view](/docs/commands#inquiry)
- **Try it:** [Open the editor](/draw), type `Z` and press Enter.

## Options [#options]

### `All` (`A`) [#all]

Frames everything drawn, as `Extents` does.

### `Extents` (`E`) [#extents]

Frames everything drawn, as large as the window allows. With nothing drawn, it says `Regenerating model.`, and typed inside another command it refuses with `** Requires a regen, cannot be transparent.`, as AutoCAD's does.

### `Previous` (`P`) [#previous]

Goes back to the view before, up to 20 views back. With none left: `No previous view saved.`.

### `Scale` (`S`) [#scale]

A factor: `2X` zooms twice as close as the current view, `0.5X` half as close, and `2` without the X twice the scale of ZOOM All's view. `nXP`, paper space's factor, is read as `nX`: there is only model space here. A factor typed at the first prompt does the same without choosing the option.

### `Window` (`W`) [#window]

Two corners, picked or dragged, frame that rectangle. A corner picked at the first prompt does the same.

### `Object` (`O`) [#object]

Frames the objects you select, or those selected before ZOOM.

## What it does not do yet [#missing]

## Questions [#questions]

### What is the shortcut for ZOOM in AutoCAD?

`Z`, then Enter or Space. The full name works too, and `_ZOOM` in any language of AutoCAD. `Z` then `E` is ZOOM Extents.

### How do I zoom to fit the whole drawing?

`Extents`: type `Z`, Enter, `E`, Enter. Or double-click the mouse wheel button.

### What does ZOOM XP mean in AutoCAD?

`nXP` zooms a layout viewport to a scale relative to paper space: `1/50XP` shows the model at 1:50 on the sheet. MellowCAD has no layouts, so `XP` is read as `X`.

### How do I zoom to an object in AutoCAD?

Choose `Object` and select it, or select it first and then run ZOOM Object: the view frames it.

### How do I go back to the previous view?

`Previous`, again and again for the views before it, up to 20.
