AREA measures the area and the perimeter of a closed shape, either one you outline by its corners or an object you pick. Give the first corner, then the next ones: from the second, the shape closed through the cursor fills in translucent green, as AutoCAD shades it. Enter or `Total` writes `Area = …, Perimeter = …` at the command prompt and ends; Enter before the third corner is refused. With Add area and Subtract area it keeps a running total over several shapes. Numbers are written at the drawing's precision, and the last area measured can be read back by typing `'AREA`.

- **Command:** `AREA`
- **Aliases:** `AA`
- **Panel:** [Inquiry & view](/docs/commands#inquiry)
- **Try it:** [Open the editor](/draw), type `AA` and press Enter.

## Options [#options]

### `Object` (`O`) [#object]

The first prompt's default, so Enter chooses it: pick a circle, an ellipse, a closed polyline or spline, a hatch or a region, and AREA writes its area with its perimeter, or a circle's circumference. An open polyline, spline or elliptical arc is closed by a straight line from end to end, and AREA writes its length. A line or a text has no area: `Selected object does not have an area`, and it asks again.

### `Add area` (`A`) [#add]

Starts a running total: each area measured after it, by corners or with `Object`, is added, and AREA writes it and the total. Enter ends the objects or the corners and asks again, until Enter at the first prompt ends.

### `Subtract area` (`S`) [#subtract]

The same, taking each area away from the total: the plate, then its holes.

### `Undo` (`U`) [#undo]

Takes back the last corner given.

### `Total` (`T`) [#total]

Writes the area and the perimeter of the corners given, and ends.

## Questions [#questions]

### What is the shortcut for AREA in AutoCAD?

`AA`, then Enter or Space. The full name works too, and `_AREA` in any language of AutoCAD.

### How do I find the area of a polyline or a hatch?

Run AREA, press Enter to choose `Object`, and pick the closed polyline or the hatch: its area and perimeter are written at the command prompt. A hatch's area leaves its islands out.

### How do I subtract an area in AutoCAD?

Choose `Add` and then `Object`, pick the outer shape and press Enter; then choose `Subtract`, `Object`, and pick each hole. AREA writes the total left after each one; Enter twice ends.

### How do I get the area in square metres?

AREA writes square drawing units. If you draw in millimetres, divide by 1,000,000: 6,000,000 is 6 m². In centimetres, by 10,000.

### Why does AREA say the object has no area?

Only closed shapes and curves have one: lines, arcs on their own, text and dimensions do not. Pick a closed polyline, a circle or a hatch, or join the lines into a polyline with [`JOIN`](/docs/commands/join) first; or outline the shape by its corners.
