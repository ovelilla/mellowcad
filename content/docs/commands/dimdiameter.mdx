DIMDIAMETER dimensions the diameter of a circle, an arc or a polyline's arc segment, with [`DIMRADIUS`](/docs/commands/dimradius)'s prompts: pick the curve, and the command line says the measurement at once; then place the dimension, and the command ends. Its text is the diameter after the diameter sign. Placed inside the circle, one line runs from the text to an arrowhead on the rim; placed outside, the line crosses the circle from rim to rim, its far arrowhead pointing back in from a short stub, as ISO-25 draws it.

- **Command:** `DIMDIAMETER`
- **Aliases:** `DIMDIA`, `DDI`
- **Panel:** [Annotate](/docs/commands#annotate)
- **Try it:** [Open the editor](/draw), type `DDI` and press Enter.

## At the command line [#session]

The engine draws it and writes its history as you would type it: the prompts in AutoCAD 2027's order, with the same option letters.

## Options [#options]

### `Text` (`T`) [#text]

Asks for the text to write instead of the measurement, which it offers as the default: Enter keeps the measurement. In what you type, `<>` stands for the measurement with its sign, so `<> THRU` writes the diameter followed by THRU.

### `Angle` (`A`) [#angle]

Asks for the angle of the text, which is then drawn at that angle, its middle on the line, and the line stops at it. 0 puts it back along the line.

## What it does not do yet [#missing]

Mtext: a dimension's text is one line, set with `Text` at the command line, never in the text editor. The sign is drawn Ø, where AutoCAD writes ∅: the drawing font has no ∅. On an arc, the arrowheads stay between its ends, where AutoCAD draws an arc extension line. As with [`DIMRADIUS`](/docs/commands/dimradius), a curve inside a block reference cannot be picked, the style's centre lines are not drawn and its settings for text outside the curve are not offered. A dimension is not associative: it does not follow the curve it measures.

## Questions [#questions]

### What is the shortcut for DIMDIAMETER in AutoCAD?

`DDI`, or `DIMDIA`, then Enter or Space. The full name works too, and `_DIMDIAMETER` in any language of AutoCAD.

### How do I dimension a small hole?

Place the text outside the circle: the dimension line then crosses the hole with an arrowhead on each side, and the text stands clear of it. Inside, the text needs room within the circle.

### Can DIMDIAMETER dimension an arc?

Yes: an arc or a polyline's arc segment, as a circle, and it reports the diameter of the arc's circle. Anything else says `Object selected is not a circle or arc.` and asks again.

### How do I add THRU or a note to a diameter dimension?

Choose `Text` before placing it and type `<> THRU`: `<>` is the measurement with its sign. Once placed, the Properties palette's Text override changes it.
