REGION turns closed shapes into regions: flat areas with no thickness, each bounded by its loops. Select the objects and press Enter; every loop they make becomes a region. A closed object is a loop of its own: a circle, an ellipse, a closed polyline or spline. Open ones — lines, arcs, elliptical arcs, open polylines and splines — are chained end to end, never where they cross, and a chain that does not close is dropped. The command line then counts the loops it found, those it rejected and why, and the regions it created; Enter on nothing says `None found.`.

The objects that went into a region are erased, the others kept, and the regions take the current layer and properties, not the objects'. A loop inside another makes a region of its own, not a hole in it: [`SUBTRACT`](/docs/commands/subtract) takes one from the other. A region draws as its edges and snaps at them; the Properties palette shows its area and perimeter, which [`AREA`](/docs/commands/area), [`LIST`](/docs/commands/list) and [`MASSPROP`](/docs/commands/massprop) measure too. [`UNION`](/docs/commands/union), [`SUBTRACT`](/docs/commands/subtract) and [`INTERSECT`](/docs/commands/intersect) combine regions in one plane, [`EXTRUDE`](/docs/commands/extrude), [`REVOLVE`](/docs/commands/revolve) and [`PRESSPULL`](/docs/commands/presspull) turn them into 3D solids, and [`EXPLODE`](/docs/commands/explode) gives back their lines, arcs and circles. The whole command is one undo step.

- **Command:** `REGION`
- **Aliases:** `REG`
- **Panel:** [Draw](/docs/commands#draw)
- **Try it:** [Open the editor](/draw), type `REG` and press Enter.

## What it does not do yet [#missing]

AutoCAD's `DELOBJ`: the objects that went into a region are always erased here. BOUNDARY, which can make a region from the area round a point, and a region from a hatch's boundary. Editing a region by its grips: they show, but dragging one changes nothing. Its edges as a cutting or boundary edge for [`TRIM`](/docs/commands/trim) and [`EXTEND`](/docs/commands/extend) in Quick mode, and [`HATCH`](/docs/commands/hatch) of a region with an elliptical or spline edge. Regions combined with surfaces, which MellowCAD does not have, and the Material row in the Properties palette.

## Questions [#questions]

### How do I use the REGION command in AutoCAD?

Run REGION (or `REG`), select the closed shapes or the lines that close one end to end, and press Enter. The session's window round four lines and a circle makes two regions, and the command line counts two loops extracted and two regions created.

### Why does REGION not create a region?

The shape is not closed end to end. Lines that cross, or that stop short of one another, close nothing: REGION chains open objects only by their ends, and a gap of a hair is enough to drop the chain. A loop that crosses itself, like a bow tie, is rejected and counted under self intersections. Join the ends with [`FILLET`](/docs/commands/fillet) at radius 0, or [`TRIM`](/docs/commands/trim) what overshoots, and try again.

### How do I make a region with a hole?

Make a region of the outline and one of the hole, then run [`SUBTRACT`](/docs/commands/subtract): pick the outline, Enter, the hole, Enter. The result is one region, and [`EXTRUDE`](/docs/commands/extrude) turns it into a solid with the hole through it.

### How do I find the area of a region?

Select it and read Area in the Properties palette, or run [`AREA`](/docs/commands/area), choose `Object` and pick it. [`MASSPROP`](/docs/commands/massprop) adds the perimeter, the centroid and the moments of inertia.
