Draw this stepped block in isometric, in millimetres: 60 long, 40 deep and 40 high, with a step 20 deep and 20 high along its front. Every edge runs along one of the isometric axes: 30° and 210° for the length, 150° and 330° for the depth, 90° and 270° for the height. MellowCAD does not have AutoCAD's isometric drafting yet, its isometric snap and isocircles, so the edges are lines typed with polar coordinates, a length and an angle.

- **Exercise:** Intermediate · Mechanical · Isometric
- **Commands:** [`LINE`](/docs/commands/line), [`DIMALIGNED`](/docs/commands/dimaligned)
- **In the editor:** [Open it with its drawing](/draw?drawing=isometric-block), dimensioned beside where you draw yours

## Step by step [#steps]

The engine draws each step and writes its history as you would type it: the prompts in AutoCAD 2027's order, with the same option letters. After a command's first point, a typed point is measured from the last one, as dynamic input measures it, and the history writes it with @; \# measures from the origin.

### 1. Draw the front of the step [#front]

On the Outline layer, [`LINE`](/docs/commands/line) from `0,0`, the bottom front corner: `60<30`, `20<90`, `60<210`, then `Close`.

<Session name="exercises/isometric-block/front" caption="1. Draw the front of the step" />

### 2. Draw the side [#side]

From `0,0` again, the left side: `40<150`, `40<90`, `20<330`, `20<270` and `20<330`. Enter ends the line on the step's front edge.

<Session name="exercises/isometric-block/side" caption="2. Draw the side" />

### 3. Draw the top [#top]

The rest starts at corners already drawn: type `_END`, the Endpoint object snap for one point, and pick the corner. From the front's top right corner: `20<150`, `20<90`, `20<150` and `60<210`. Then two lines of `60<210`, from the riser's foot and from its top.

<Session name="exercises/isometric-block/top" caption="3. Draw the top" />

### 4. Dimension it [#dimensions]

On Dimensions, [`DIMALIGNED`](/docs/commands/dimaligned) along the edges, each placed beside its own: 60 and 40 at the bottom, 40 up the left, 20 up the right.
