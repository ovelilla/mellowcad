## How the exercises work [#how]

An exercise opens in the editor with its drawing, dimensioned, left of the origin: draw yours from 0,0, as its solution does. What you draw is kept in this browser for that exercise alone. The drawing you were working on stays as it was, and the editor opened on its own brings it back. To start an exercise again, run NEW, then open it from its page. Every page prints on its own, the drawing first and the steps after, and your browser's print dialog saves it as a PDF.

## Questions [#questions]

### Where can I find AutoCAD exercises to practise?

Here: each exercise has its drawing dimensioned, the commands it practises and its solution step by step, and opens in a free editor that works like AutoCAD, in the browser. Start with the beginner ones; each command they use leads to what it does and the options it asks for.

### Can I practise AutoCAD online for free?

Yes. MellowCAD runs in the browser, free and with no account: you type AutoCAD's commands and aliases, in your language's names, and answer their prompts in the same order, with the same option letters. It is not AutoCAD: it opens DXF files but cannot save them or open DWG yet, and its 3D solids are still partial.

### Do the exercises have solutions?

Every exercise is solved step by step under its drawing. The engine draws each step and writes the command line's history, so you can compare yours with it line by line.

### Can I print the exercises or save them as a PDF?

Yes. An exercise's page prints on its own, dark lines on white, the drawing first and the steps after, and your browser's print dialog saves it as a PDF.
