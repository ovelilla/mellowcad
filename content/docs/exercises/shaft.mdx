Draw this stepped shaft in side view, in millimetres: 150 long, with diameters of 30, 40 and 30 over lengths of 40, 60 and 50, and both ends chamfered 2 by 2. Draw the half above the axis as one polyline, chamfer it and mirror it; then the edges seen across the shaft, the axis and the lengths. The diameters are the ones given here.

## Step by step [#steps]

The engine draws each step and writes its history as you would type it: the prompts in AutoCAD 2027's order, with the same option letters. After a command's first point, a typed point is measured from the last one, as dynamic input measures it, and the history writes it with @; \# measures from the origin.

### 1. Draw the upper half [#half]

On the Outline layer, one [`PLINE`](/docs/commands/pline) from `0,0`: up `0,15`, along `40,0`, up `0,5`, along `60,0`, down `0,-5`, along `50,0` and down `0,-15`, back to the axis. Enter ends it.

<Session name="exercises/shaft/half" caption="1. Draw the upper half" />

### 2. Chamfer the ends [#chamfers]

[`CHAMFER`](/docs/commands/chamfer), then `Distance`: `2`, and Enter takes `2` for the second distance too. `Multiple` chamfers one corner after another without starting again: pick the two segments at each end's corner.

<Session name="exercises/shaft/chamfers" caption="2. Chamfer the ends" />

### 3. Mirror it [#mirror]

[`MIRROR`](/docs/commands/mirror): pick the polyline, press Enter, then the mirror line from `0,0` to `150,0`, the axis, and Enter to keep the original.

<Session name="exercises/shaft/mirror" caption="3. Mirror it" />

### 4. Draw the edges [#edges]

With [`LINE`](/docs/commands/line), the edges seen across the shaft, each `0,30` up from the bottom: from `2,-15` and `148,-15` at the chamfers, and from `40,-15` and `100,-15` at the shoulders.

<Session name="exercises/shaft/edges" caption="4. Draw the edges" />

### 5. Draw the axis [#axis]

On Centre lines, the axis: a line from `-8,0` to `166,0` further on.

<Session name="exercises/shaft/axis" caption="5. Draw the axis" />

### 6. Dimension it [#dimensions]

On Dimensions, [`DIMLINEAR`](/docs/commands/dimlinear) for the first length, 40, placed below. [`DIMCONTINUE`](/docs/commands/dimcontinue) carries on from its second extension line: `#100,-20`, then `#150,-15`, typed from the origin with #, and Enter twice to end it. A last [`DIMLINEAR`](/docs/commands/dimlinear) gives the whole length, 150, further below.
