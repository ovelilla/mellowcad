Model this pulley by revolving its half section round its axis, in millimetres: a bore of 20, a hub 30 long and 40 across, a web 10 thick and a rim 24 wide from 90 to 110 across, centred on the hub. The section is drawn as distances from the axis, which runs up the Y axis, so each diameter is drawn as its half.

- **Exercise:** Intermediate · Mechanical · 3D solid
- **Commands:** [`PLINE`](/docs/commands/pline), [`-VIEW`](/docs/commands#-view), [`VSCURRENT`](/docs/commands/vscurrent), [`REVOLVE`](/docs/commands/revolve), [`ZOOM`](/docs/commands/zoom)

## Step by step [#steps]

The engine draws each step and writes its history as you would type it: the prompts in AutoCAD 2027's order, with the same option letters. After a command's first point, a typed point is measured from the last one, as dynamic input measures it, and the history writes it with @; \# measures from the origin.

### 1. Draw the half section [#profile]

On the Outline layer, one closed [`PLINE`](/docs/commands/pline) from `10,0`, each point after it a step from the last: `10,0`, `0,10`, `25,0`, `0,-7`, `10,0`, `0,24`, `-10,0`, `0,-7`, `-25,0`, `0,10` and `-10,0`, then `Close`.

<Session name="exercises/pulley/profile" caption="1. Draw the half section" />

### 2. Turn the view [#view]

`-VIEW` and `SEIso` look at it from the south-east and above; [`VSCURRENT`](/docs/commands/vscurrent) and `Conceptual` shade the faces, which shows a turned part best.

<Session name="exercises/pulley/view" caption="2. Turn the view" />

### 3. Revolve it [#revolve]

[`REVOLVE`](/docs/commands/revolve): pick the section and press Enter, then `Y`: the Y axis is the pulley's. Enter takes the default angle, a whole turn. [`ZOOM`](/docs/commands/zoom) `Extents` frames it, and a scale of 0.8x leaves room round it.
