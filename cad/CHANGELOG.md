## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

Drawing file version 41: an older file's rectangular array reads as one level, 1 apart.
- 1afba9d: How the history writes an answer after a prompt is the language's: the new `commands.answerForms` says, by prompt id, whether it goes past the colon's space (`[Multiple]: M`) or against the colon where the language's prompt ends without one, with `format.answeredTight`, and `EngineText.answerForm` reads it. DIMSPACE's prompts and -DIMSTYLE's redefine now write `Select base dimension:50,-40` in every language, as their consoles do; Brazilian Portuguese writes PEDIT's, MREDO's, ARRAYPATH's main prompt and ARRAYRECT's rows at Spacing the same way, and Spanish the unit cell. ARRAYPATH's main prompt (`selectGripToEditPathArrayOr`) and ARRAYRECT's rows at Spacing (`specifyDistanceBetweenRowsOfCell`) have prompt ids of their own; Brazilian Portuguese words Rows' `Especificar a distância entre linhas`, and its Ortho and Grid toggles `<Ortogonal ativado>` and `<Grade ativado>`, as its desktop does.
- 4c31ea2: OPEN opens a DXF and DXFIN (es CARGADXF, pt-BR IMPDXF) opens one alone, as AutoCAD 2027 does: ASCII or binary, from R12 to 2018, the text in its code page up to 2004, with lines, circles, arcs, ellipses, polylines (PEDIT's fitted ones as AutoCAD left them), 3D polylines, splines, TEXT, MTEXT, attribute definitions, block references with their attributes, the linear, aligned, angular, radius and diameter dimensions and hatches, on their layers with their colours, linetypes, lineweights and styles. What the engine has no object for is left out and counted on the command line (`7 objects not opened: 1 POINT, …`), and a file that is no DXF, or ends inside a section, is refused with AutoCAD's own lines. `FileTransport.open` takes the kinds a pick offers (`OpenRequest`, `FileKind`) and may return the file's name and bytes (`OpenedFile`), which a binary DXF needs; a transport returning text still works. `CadEngine.openDrawingFromFile` takes the kinds too. New catalog keys: `interface.description.DXFIN`, `commands.names.DXFIN`, the `dxf*` messages; in pt-BR, IMPDXF moves from OPEN's aliases to DXFIN's name.
- 10d28c9: French prompts are worded per command where French AutoCAD words them so: new prompt ids (`alignedSecondExtensionOrigin`, `polygonFirstEndpointOfEdge`, `polygonSecondEndpointOfEdge`, `enterThirdCvPointOr`, `peditMultipleOption`), the labels French lists otherwise at one prompt (SPLINE's `Tangence fin`, EDITSPLINE's `Par Défaut`, PEDIT Multiple's `Typeligne gén`), and the answers French writes against the colon (the arrays' counts, spacings, type, method and alignment, COTANG's first prompt, SPLINE's third CV point). LIST words a fitted polyline's POLYLINE and VERTEX lines with keys apart from an LWPOLYLINE's (`listPolylineStartingWidth`, `listPolylineEndingWidth`, `listVertexEndingWidth`, `listVertexBulge`, `listVertexStartAngle`, `listVertexEndAngle`, `listLwRadius`), which French writes a column apart and in words of their own; the other languages read as before.
- 56d4d08: `@mellowcad/engine/locales/fr`, French's catalog, is an entry point: its interface and the command language of French AutoCAD 2027 (`LIGNE`, `DECALER`, `[Clore/annUler]`).
- 4fa4daf: MASSPROP, as AutoCAD 2027 reports it: a set's solids and regions, each summed as one body, in the current UCS — mass, volume, bounding box, centroid, moments and products of inertia, radii of gyration and the principal moments with their directions; a region off the XY plane, its area, perimeter, box and centroid —, word for word in English, Spanish (PROPFIS) and Brazilian Portuguese (PROPMASS), then `Write analysis to a file?`, whose Yes hands an `.mpr` file to the `FileTransport` (`save` receives it as it receives a drawing; the browser's names its download's type by the extension). The kernel integrates every face exactly. The editor's rail leaves MASSPROP out, as AutoCAD's ribbon does: it is typed. New catalog keys: `interface.tool.massprop`, `commands.names.MASSPROP`, `commands.keywords.MASSPROP`, `commands.prompts.massPropWriteFile`, the `massProp*` messages.
- cc5435a: How a prompt writes its default is the language's: the new `commands.promptForms` says, by prompt id, whether it is spaced (`<0>:`), against what precedes it (`[Zdepth]<0,0,0>:`) or without a colon (`OK? <Y>`), and `EngineText.promptForm` reads it. Brazilian Portuguese spaces UCS's and -VIEW's defaults (`[profundidadeZ] <0,0,0>:`), and the arrays' main prompt now writes `…/eXit]<eXit>:` in English and Brazilian Portuguese, as their consoles do, and `…/Salir] <Salir>:` in Spanish. The new `commands.promptKeywordLabels` gives a keyword the label one prompt lists it with, before its command's: Brazilian Portuguese UCS New lists `EIxo z`.
- 5b58d00: `@mellowcad/engine/locales/pt-BR` gains its command language, Brazilian Portuguese AutoCAD 2027's: command names and aliases (`LINHA`, `CI`, `DESLOCAMENTO`, `D` for U), prompts, keywords and messages, checked against its console. The language is still in progress: some prompts English shares between commands are worded per command in Brazilian Portuguese and take one wording for now.
- b48a1b5: Brazilian Portuguese prompts are worded per command where AutoCAD words them so: new prompt ids (`plineNextPointOr`, `specifyNextPointOrTotal`, `addModeNextPointOrTotal`, `subtractModeNextPointOrTotal`, `textJustifyOption`, `ucsiconOption`, `navvcubeOption`, `areaFirstCornerPointOr`, `plineCenterPointOfArc`, `copyBasePointOr`, `copyDisplacement`, `copyUseFirstPoint`, `selectEdgesOr`), keywords spelled per prompt and the labels pt-BR lists otherwise. Its object snaps are typed by their three capitals, so `A`, `M` or `C` at a point prompt is the keyword again. -OSNAP lists the running modes by AutoCAD's short names in OSMODE's order in every language (`Quad,Int,Ins,Perp,Near,App Int,Ext,Par`), from the new message `osnapModeName`. `EngineText.keywordLabel` takes the prompt, where a label that only repeats the command's word gives way to that prompt's own. Spanish UCSICON's Noorigin is the word its console declares, `Sin`, listed `Sin origen`.
- ece5a3d: `@mellowcad/engine/locales/pt-BR` (exported as `ptBR`), Brazilian Portuguese, is an entry point. It is in progress: its interface is Brazilian Portuguese AutoCAD's, and until its command language is written the command line speaks the global one, as a language without `commands` does.
- 8e5507f: A solid that UNION, SUBTRACT or INTERSECT made shows one grip, as AutoCAD 2027 does, at its box's X-Y centre and lowest Z, which moves it whole. The Properties palette's drop-down names one object alone by its type, without the count (`Line`, not `Line (1)`): `EngineText.propertiesFilter`'s count is optional. The editor's command line hints `Type a command` only at `Command:`, not while a command asks.
- 40a53e2: REGION, UNION, SUBTRACT and INTERSECT of regions, as AutoCAD 2027 asks and reports them, on the engine's own planar kernel (exact edges: lines, circular and elliptical arcs, NURBS pieces; one tolerance, 1e-6). A new entity type, `RegionEntity` (`"region"` in `EntityType`): faces of loops of edges in the plane of its normal, drawn, picked and snapped by its edges, with grips at its corners and a whole loop's centre; the palette shows Area and Perimeter (`perimeter`), LIST and AREA report it, EXPLODE breaks it up. Drawing file version 42. The editor's tool palette gains Region in Draw and the Solid Editing drop-down (Union, Subtract, Intersect) in Modify.
- 3d5a9f7: UNION, SUBTRACT and INTERSECT of 3D solids, as AutoCAD 2027 combines them: a set's solids become its first (its object and properties kept), regions alongside them plane by plane; SUBTRACT joins the first set's solids and takes the second's out; solids apart unite into one of several shells; an empty result says `Null solid created - deleted` and erases them; one undo step. The combined solid has no history: LIST shows `History = None`, `Show History = No` and its bounding box. The engine's own B-rep kernel intersects planes, cylinders, cones, spheres, tori and the swept surfaces in their closed forms, and measures every face exactly, a curved one by Green's theorem over its loops; a pair it has no closed form for is refused with AutoCAD's `1 boolean operation(s) failed.`, the solids left as they were. New catalog keys: the `nullSolid` and `booleanFailed` messages.
- f22e1ae: The primitive solids' grips, as AutoCAD 2027 lays them: a square at the base's centre that moves the solid; a box's and a wedge's base corners and edge arrows, a pyramid's corners and sides, a cylinder's and a cone's base quadrants, a sphere's equator, a torus' ring and tube; off the plan, the height's arrows and a pyramid's or cone's top radius. A drag sizes the solid along its arrow (in the base's plane, or along the line of sight), the box typing how far or, Tab's, the size; a drag AutoCAD leaves the solid by is refused; one undo step. Their Geometry rows in the Properties palette are typed — Position, Length, Width, Height, the radii, Sides, Diameter, Torus and Tube radius —, with AutoCAD's refusals, a negative height turning the solid over. `EntityProperty` gains `SolidProperty`'s rows (`solidLength`, `solidWidth`, `solidHeight`, `baseRadius`, `topRadius`, `sides`, the elliptical radii, `torusRadius`, `tubeRadius`).
- ff49313: BOX, as AutoCAD 2027 asks it — two corners, Center, Cube, Length, the height or 2Point, its defaults and refusals — making the engine's first 3D solid. A new entity type, `Solid3dEntity` (`"solid3d"` in `EntityType`): a B-rep of the engine's own kernel (vertices, edges with their curve, faces with their surface and loops of coedges) in the WCS, keeping the box it was made as (`BoxPrimitive`); drawn in 2D Wireframe as its edges, picked and snapped by them, carried by MOVE, COPY, ROTATE, SCALE, MIRROR and STRETCH, listed by LIST, measured by AREA, exploded into regions. The palette shows its Geometry and Solid History (`solidType`, `width`, `history`, `showHistory` rows; the `solidHistory` group). `DocumentSettings.isolines` (ISOLINES, saved with the drawing). Drawing file version 43. The editor's tool palette gains Box in Draw.
- 186a944: CYLINDER and CONE, as AutoCAD 2027 asks them: the base by its centre and radius or diameter, 3P, 2P, Ttr or Elliptical, the height, 2Point or Axis endpoint, CONE's Top radius, with their refusals and shared defaults. The solid kernel gains curved geometry: an edge's `BrepCurve` may be a `circle`, an `ellipse` or an apex's `point`, a face's `BrepSurface` a `cylinder` or a `cone`; area and volume are exact for them, and `brepMapped` takes the map's scale. `CylinderPrimitive` and `ConePrimitive` join `SolidPrimitive`. A solid draws ISOLINES' rulings on its curved faces and is picked and windowed by them; it snaps at its circles' centres and quadrants and a cone's apex. EXPLODE refuses a solid with a curved face (no surface object yet). Drawing file version 45. The editor's Modeling drop-down gains Cylinder and Cone.
- 5b36612: EXTRUDE, as AutoCAD 2027 asks it: the set of closed profiles (circles, ellipses, closed polylines and splines, regions; what it cannot sweep sifted out as Enter closes it), MOde, then the height or Direction, Taper angle and Expression; each profile becomes a solid of the current layer, its sides the faces ACIS makes (planes, cylinders, cones, elliptical cylinders, extrusions of splines), exact in volume and MASSPROP; DELOBJ erases, keeps or asks about the profiles. - 545fb31: SLICE (SL) and SECTION (SEC), PRESSPULL on a solid's faces and a solid's faces, edges and vertices selected with Ctrl+click, as AutoCAD 2027 does them — in English, Spanish (CORTE, SECCION) and Brazilian Portuguese (FATIAR, CORTE). ## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- f480eac: 3DPOLY draws a 3D polyline, a new entity type (`Polyline3dEntity`, `type: "polyline3d"`) whose vertices each keep their own Z: a host switching over `Entity["type"]` gains a case. The editor's palette offers it beside the splines, as the ribbon's expanded Draw panel does.
- 118bbdb: F1 is AutoCAD's Help: the engine asks its host for the help of the running command, by its global name, or for the help's start with none (`CadEngineOptions.onHelp`, `CadDriverOptions.help`); `CadCanvas` hands `onHelp` on, and `CadEditor` opens its `help` links in a new tab. Without a help to show, F1 stays the browser's.
- 8cbb6b6: SPLINE keeps each point's Z, as AutoCAD does: a spline may now lie in no plane. `SplineEntity`'s `points`, `startTangent` and `endTangent` are `Vec3`s (in the object coordinates of its normal; a spline has no `elevation` of its own), so a host that builds one by hand gives them a `z`. One whose points lie in a tilted plane is kept in that plane, its normal that plane's. Drawing files are version 37; older ones load as they were.
- eda1bd1: SPLINEDIT (SPE) edits a spline as AutoCAD 2027 does: Close / Open, Join, Fit data (Add, Close / Open, Delete, Move, Purge, Tangents), Edit vertex (Add, Delete, Elevate order up to 26, Move, Weight), convert to Polyline, Reverse and an Undo of its own per step, each run one undo step. The editor's rail gains Edit Spline in the Modify panel.

### Patch Changes

- a9d7383: The engine's documentation: a README to start from and five guides beside it (`docs/concepts.md`, `host.md`, `drawings.md`, `headless.md`, `languages.md`), and the public methods of `CadEngine` documented for an integrator, as an editor's hover shows them.
- 3755cc8: TRIM, EXTEND, BREAK and BREAKATPOINT edit a spline as AutoCAD 2027 does, on its own parameter: TRIM's piece is a CV spline on the knots and control points it had there, a broken or extended fit spline stays a fit spline, a closed one opens into one piece across its seam, and a spline in space or at an angle to the UCS is cut in plan. Each edit is one undo step and keeps the spline's properties.
- d673436: JOIN joins splines and elliptical arcs as AutoCAD 2027 does: a spline with lines, arcs, polylines, other splines or elliptical arcs that meet its ends becomes one CV spline on each object's own curve, in space too; elliptical arcs of one ellipse become one arc, or the whole ellipse; an arc and an elliptical arc become a new spline. A spline or an elliptical arc picked alone is a source the next objects join, and cLose closes an elliptical arc into its ellipse. Each JOIN is one undo step.
- c22ccae: A spline may carry knots of its own and, on control vertices, weights: `SplineEntity` gains the optional `knotVector` (DXF 40, as AutoCAD holds it, never normalised) and `weights` (DXF 41), which the editing of splines will leave on its pieces. A fit spline with a `knotVector` reads Knot parameterization Custom; a CV spline with `weights` is rational and the palette shows each vertex's Weight. Drawing files are version 38; older ones load as they were.
- 11e075f: OFFSET takes an ellipse, an elliptical arc and a spline lying in a plane, as AutoCAD 2027 does: the copy is a CV spline on the source's own parameter within 0.001 of the exact offset, cut where an inward offset crosses itself, and a round ellipse's a concentric ellipse. Through, Erase, Layer and Multiple work as with any object; each offset is one undo step, and the copy keeps the source's layer, colour and linetype but, as in AutoCAD, not its lineweight.
